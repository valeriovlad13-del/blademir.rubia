Smart Energy Meter

A fabricated IoT-based energy monitoring system using an ESP32 microcontroller and PZEM-004T sensor for real-time residential electricity monitoring, local display, remote Blynk monitoring, estimated billing, and hourly email notifications.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

From electrical measurements to connected energy monitoring.

System Purpose

The project was designed and fabricated to provide real-time monitoring of residential electricity use. The PZEM-004T measures electrical parameters while the ESP32 processes the readings and connects the system to its monitoring interfaces.

Energy Monitoring ESP32 PZEM-004T IoT

Local + Remote Monitoring

A 16x2 I2C LCD provides local readings, while the Blynk application provides remote monitoring through Wi-Fi. The system also sends hourly email updates containing energy consumption and estimated billing information.

Blynk monitoring and estimated billing

The thesis demonstrates a Blynk interface for viewing voltage, current, power, energy, and estimated bill. The example shown in the study records 223.7 V, 0.66 A, 116.8 W, and 1.71 kWh.

The study explains that real power is not simply voltage multiplied by current because AC power depends on power factor. For its example, the estimated bill uses 1.71 kWh and the MASELCO rate stated in the thesis, ₱10.625/kWh.

Study example: Estimated bill = 1.71 kWh × ₱10.625/kWh ≈ ₱18.17

LIMITATIONS & FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

What the study identified for the next iteration.

Study limitations

  • Final evaluation was limited to a 12-hour data collection period.
  • The study did not cover power quality, network latency, long-term durability, or cybersecurity.
  • The notification reliability evaluation used a small sample of 12 hourly observations.

Recommendations in the research

  • Upgrade to Blynk Pro for expanded functionality and data management.
  • Integrate Firebase for long-term storage and historical trend analysis.
  • Test the device under varying load conditions.
  • Increase the sample size for stronger statistical power.
