Residential Electrical Systems Design

A residential electrical design project completed for EE412 – Electrical Systems and Illumination Engineering Design at DEBESMSCAT. The work covers both single-family and multi-family dwelling applications.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

From floor plans to electrical distribution.

Design Scope

Developed residential floor plans together with lighting layouts, power layouts, schedules of loads, single-line diagrams, circuit arrangements, conductor and conduit specifications, and bills of materials.

Electrical Design Load Analysis Power Distribution Technical Drafting

Design Applications

The project includes a 10 m × 10 m single-family dwelling design and a multi-family dwelling design developed on a per-unit basis with a sub-feeder arrangement.

Single-Family Multi-Family Panel Boards Residential Circuits
01 — SINGLE-FAMILY DWELLING

Residential electrical design package

The single-family package contains the floor plan, lighting layout, power layout, and schedule of loads with a single-line diagram.

02 — MULTI-FAMILY DWELLING

Per-unit electrical design and distribution

The multi-family package extends the residential design to dwelling units and includes a sub-feeder arrangement in the distribution design.
